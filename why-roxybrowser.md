## The problem RoxyBrowser actually solves

A normal browser leaks a consistent "fingerprint" — a blend of your user agent, screen resolution, timezone, installed fonts, canvas and WebGL rendering, and dozens of smaller signals. Two accounts opened from the same machine share most of that fingerprint, which is exactly how platforms detect that one person is running many "different" identities.

An antidetect browser like RoxyBrowser gives each profile its own isolated fingerprint **and** its own cookie/storage container. Profile A looks like a different device from Profile B, and neither can see the other's session data.

## What makes it my top pick

- **Genuinely usable free tier.** RoxyBrowser's free plan includes 5 permanent profiles that don't expire. Most competitors trial-gate you or expire free profiles after a few days, so you can test a real workflow before paying anything.
- **Fingerprints that hold together.** The details that matter — timezone, WebGL, canvas, fonts — stay internally consistent per profile, which is what keeps a profile from looking "spoofed."
- **Proxy handling is per-profile.** You bind a proxy to each profile, so your IP location and your fingerprint's timezone can be matched deliberately instead of contradicting each other.
- **Workflow-first design.** Features like syncing actions across multiple windows are built for people actually running several accounts at once, not just for hiding.
